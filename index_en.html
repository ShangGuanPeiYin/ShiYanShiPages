About MVAS

Welcome to the Machine Vision and Autonomous Systems (MVAS) Laboratory! Our research focuses on machine perception, decision-making, and control. We deeply integrate Computer Vision with Autonomous Control Algorithms to address the challenges of perception, planning, and safety for autonomous systems in complex environments.

We provide safe, reliable, and efficient software power for autonomous systems and translate them into high-value commercial products, such as Unmanned Road Sweepers, Autonomous Lawnmowers, and Intelligent Ship Berthing Assistance Systems.

Led by Prof. Junguo Lu, the MVAS Lab is part of the School of Electronic Information and Electrical Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. We host over 30 dynamic researchers and engineers. If you are interested in joining us, please visit our Careers page.
